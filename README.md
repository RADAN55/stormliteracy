# Storm Literacy · The Teacher Literacy Lab

**Live:** https://radan55.github.io/stormliteracy/

For EL and content teachers. Paste a reading or upload a PDF and get the academic vocabulary with Spanish, the cognates, the hard sentences with how to teach each one, a 10-minute lesson (press Teach it and read the screen), and a student handout in Emerging and Progressing versions.

Also inside:
- **Word Check** — a vocabulary analyzer (everyday / school / subject words, cognates, false friends, word parts, word wall)
- **Text X-Ray** — marks long sentences, passive voice, process nouns, logic words and pointing words; teacher moves tagged to the MS ELP (ELPA21) and MS CCRS literacy standards
- **Juicy Sentence Studio** — 144 original sentences, one a week for 36 weeks in Biology I, U.S. History, Algebra I and English II, in English and Spanish; printable card PDFs; student link; Monday Moves card
- **The method on one page** — Read it. Break it. Make it. (after Lily Wong Fillmore and Charles Fillmore)
- **The Teacher Writes Too** — a 12-session PLC with present mode, facilitator kit PDF and certificate

Single-file `index.html`. No account, no server; PDFs are read in the browser with the bundled pdf.js (`vendor/`). English and Spanish interface. Prints in black and white.

A copy also lives on the EL Publishing hub: https://radan55.github.io/everyteacher/literacy-lab/
